Data comes straight from archive.org’s public APIs (Advanced Search, Wayback Availability, CDX) in your browser. Requests are only sent when you press Search, run one at a time, are spaced at least a second apart, and are cached for the session. The status pill only reflects requests you trigger (plus one tiny probe if you click it). Saved searches and the reading list stay in this browser’s localStorage — use Export to back them up. Press ? for keyboard shortcuts. For uptime history and the best times to archive, see the status dashboard. Results link out to archive.org — nothing is mirrored. If you rely on it heavily, consider donating to the Archive.
