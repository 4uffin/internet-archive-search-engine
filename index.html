Archive Seeksearch the Internet Archive & Wayback Machine · unofficial
Data comes straight from archive.org’s public APIs (Advanced Search, Wayback Availability, CDX) in your browser. Requests are only sent when you press Search, run one at a time, are spaced at least a second apart, and are cached for the session. Results link out to archive.org — nothing is mirrored. If you rely on it heavily, consider donating to the Archive.
